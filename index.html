Project work

Three Salesforce CPQ projects from my time at TEN Canada, each with the problem, what I did, and how it turned out.

CPQ credit approval

Salesforce CPQApproval processesFlow
Problem
Quotes for customers on credit had to be checked by Finance before they went out, and the check happened by email with no clear record of who approved what.
What I did
Built a multi-level credit approval in CPQ. Rules check the customer’s credit position and the quote value, then route to the right approver, send email alerts and keep the quote on hold until approved.
Result
Credit checks now happen inside the quote with a full approval history, so Sales and Finance spend less time chasing each other.

CPQ product configuration, renewals and amendments

Product bundlesPricing rulesRenewalsAmendmentsUpsell and downsell
Problem
Changing a customer’s contract mid-term, or renewing it, was done by hand. Price increases, upsells and downsells had to be worked out manually, which caused errors and rework.
What I did
Configured products and bundles with pricing rules and discount schedules. Built the renewal and amendment process in CPQ so the system calculates the new price, applies the renewal price increase, and handles upsell (adding products) and downsell (removing or reducing products) on the existing contract. Added quote templates and DocuSign for signing.
Result
Renewals and amendments are priced consistently from the contract, with less manual calculation and rework for Sales.

Case management for online credit cases

Service CloudWeb-to-CaseQueuesFlow
Problem
Credit requests that came in online were handled outside Salesforce, so cases were slow to route and hard to track against the related quote.
What I did
Set up case management in Service Cloud for online credit cases: intake through Web-to-Case, a dedicated record type, queues and assignment rules, status flow and email alerts, linked to the customer and the CPQ quote.
Result
Credit cases are routed to the right team on arrival and tracked in one place, so the credit team and Sales can see the same status.
